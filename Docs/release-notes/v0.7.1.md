**Download: [Wildreach-Setup.exe](https://github.com/RavenArcher17/Survival-Game/releases/latest/download/Wildreach-Setup.exe)** (1.7 GB, Windows 10/11, 64-bit)

Already have Wildreach? Just start it from its shortcut: it updates itself to 0.7.1 and your saves are kept. From 0.6.1 or later it only downloads what changed, about 235 MB; from 0.6.0 or earlier it's the whole game this one last time. New here? Double-click the installer: it installs to your user folder, no admin needed, adds Desktop and Start Menu shortcuts and starts the game. To remove it, use "Uninstall Wildreach" in the Start Menu.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Everyone in a game needs the same version, so make sure your friends update too.

## New in 0.7.1: no more console for clans and door codes

- **The Clan tab** (press **O**, or click Clan in the journal). Found a clan, invite the players in your game with one click (or anyone by name, even if they're not on), accept or turn down invites, see who's in it and who's on, remove members if you lead it, take back an invite, and leave (it asks first). When you're invited, removed or someone joins, you're told. Clanmates share bases (claims, chests, doors, stations, the Hearth) and beds, can't hurt each other, and a raid warns all of you.
- **A keypad on code-lock doors** (the reinforced door). Press E on someone else's locked door to enter its code: the right one opens it, and it's yours to open from then on until its code changes. On your own door (or your clan's), **hold E** for the keypad to set a code and lock it, or remove the lock; a tap of E still opens and shuts it. Type the digits or click them, then Enter.

Both used to need the console (`WRClan`, `WRSetCode`, `WRCode`); they still work there too.

## Console (playtest builds)

Press the **`** key (left of 1, under Esc) for the console: for example `WRGive wood 50`, `WRHour 12`, `WRSpawn cinderfang 1`, `WRCmd raid now` (a raid on the nearest claim). In this playtest build anyone in the game can use it. README.txt beside the game lists more.

## Playing with friends

1. Start Wildreach, type a name, pick Male or Female, and Fire (volcanic) or Frost (tundra).
2. One person clicks **Host a game** and chooses a world. Everyone else types the host's IP and clicks **Join**.
   - Same Wi-Fi: the host's local IP (`ipconfig`, "IPv4 Address").
   - Over the internet: the host forwards UDP port 7777 on their router to their PC and shares their public IP.
3. Every key can be changed in Esc > Controls, and H shows them in game. To start: LMB / R attack, RMB block, C dodge, E interact (hold E on your tame for orders, on your code-lock door for its keypad), 1–8 items, hold Q for abilities, Tab lock on, I J P K M O the journal, B build, X demolish, G a ranged weapon (RMB aims), Esc the menu, F10 back to the main menu.
