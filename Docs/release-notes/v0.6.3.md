**Download: [Wildreach-Setup.exe](https://github.com/RavenArcher17/Survival-Game/releases/latest/download/Wildreach-Setup.exe)** (1.7 GB, Windows 10/11, 64-bit)

Already have Wildreach? Just start it from its shortcut: it updates itself to 0.6.3 and your saves are kept. From 0.6.1 or 0.6.2 it only downloads what changed, about 235 MB, and from 0.6.2 it comes down over four connections at once; from 0.6.0 or earlier it's the whole game this one last time. New here? Double-click the installer: it installs to your user folder, no admin needed, adds Desktop and Start Menu shortcuts and starts the game. To remove it, use "Uninstall Wildreach" in the Start Menu.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Everyone in a game needs the same version, so make sure your friends update too.

## New in 0.6.3: your base

- **The Hearth and the land claim totem have their own Claim tab in the build bar.** They were the last two of eight cards on the Stations tab, and the bar only had room for three, so they sat out of sight past a scroll bar you could barely see.
- **Every build bar tab now pages its cards.** Round arrows on the ends of the cards (or the mouse wheel over them) turn to the next three, and a tag says where you are ("4–6 of 6"). Several tabs went on past the third card without showing it: the stone walls, floors and roofs among them. The tab names shrink to fit on narrower screens.
- **A hint after your first claim totem** says where the Hearth is and what it's for.
- **A broken Hearth can be repaired.** Press E on it and click **Repair**: it costs up to half of what it took to build, less the less it's damaged. Before, a Hearth knocked down to nothing was destroyed along with the cores you'd fed it. Now it stands, broken, and its stations stop until you mend it; its cores are never lost.
- **Name your chests.** **Rename** beside the chest's name opens a box; Enter or Save keeps the name, an empty name gives it its own name back. Only its owner and their clan can.
- **Waypoints.** Right-click on the map to set one; right-click it again to clear it. It shows on the map and on the compass at the top of the screen with its distance, at the compass's end with an arrow when it's behind you.
- **Your base stays on the map from anywhere.** Your Hearth and claim now show on your map however far away you are, for friends who join your game too (before, theirs dropped off past about 150 m). Other people's claims still only show when you're near them.

## Console (playtest builds)

Press the **`** key (left of 1, under Esc) for the console: for example `WRGive wood 50`, `WRHour 12`, `WRSpawn cinderfang 1`. In this playtest build anyone in the game can use it. README.txt beside the game lists more.

## Playing with friends

1. Start Wildreach, type a name, pick Male or Female, and Fire (volcanic) or Frost (tundra).
2. One person clicks **Host a game** and chooses a world. Everyone else types the host's IP and clicks **Join**.
   - Same Wi-Fi: the host's local IP (`ipconfig`, "IPv4 Address").
   - Over the internet: the host forwards UDP port 7777 on their router to their PC and shares their public IP.
3. Every key can be changed in Esc > Controls, and H shows them in game. To start: LMB / R attack, RMB block, C dodge, E interact (hold E on your tame for orders), 1–8 items, hold Q for abilities, Tab lock on, I J P K M the journal, B build, X demolish, G a ranged weapon (RMB aims), Esc the menu, F10 back to the main menu.
