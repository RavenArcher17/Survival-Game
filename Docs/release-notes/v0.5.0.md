**Download: [Wildreach-Setup.exe](https://github.com/RavenArcher17/Survival-Game/releases/latest/download/Wildreach-Setup.exe)** (1.7 GB, Windows 10/11, 64-bit)

Already have Wildreach? Just start it from its shortcut: it updates itself to 0.5.0 (your saves are kept). New here? Double-click the installer: it installs in about 20 seconds (to your user folder, no admin needed), adds Desktop and Start Menu shortcuts and starts the game. To remove it, use "Uninstall Wildreach" in the Start Menu.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Everyone in a game needs the same version, so make sure your friends update too.

## New in 0.5.0: the feedback pass

Everything here comes from playtest feedback.

- **Water you can swim in.** Walk off a beach and swim; tread water with your head out; walk back up the bank. Press **E** in the water or at its edge to drink. Creatures wade into the shallows but won't follow you out to sea.
- **Hold your tools.** Put the hatchet or pick in your hand from the hotbar (1–8) to get its bonus: a hatchet on trees, a pick on stone and ore. A sword or bare hands gathers at the basic rate. Tools now wear out (shown on the hotbar like your sword), stop giving their bonus at zero, and can be repaired at a workbench.
- **Harvest what you hunt.** A creature you kill stays down for three minutes. Hit the body to cut meat, hide and the rest from it; a blade (hatchet or sword) gets the most. The killer and their clan have it to themselves for 30 seconds.
- **See attacks coming.** Every creature now winds up before it attacks: it plants its feet, rears back, glows red-orange and gives a deep growl, so you can block or dodge.
- **Attack from your guard.** While holding block, attack swings straight away; keep holding block and your guard comes back when the swing ends.
- **A starter spell.** Your path's first spell is yours from the start (Fire bolt or Frost shard), already on your ability wheel: tap **Q** to cast it and start levelling your path. If you had bought it, you get those points back.
- **Demolish.** Press **X** in build mode (or click Demolish on the build bar): the piece you aim at glows red and shows what you get back; click to take it down. Only the person who built a piece can demolish it.
- **Caves you can walk into.** All six caves are open, lit inside, with ore you can mine.
- **Real lava.** The lava river is a dark crusted flow with glowing cracks that lies on the ground, and it only burns where you can see it.
- **A fuller island.** About three times the bushes and twice the wildlife. Resource spots look like what they are (ore boulders, sulfur crystals, berry bushes) instead of coloured cones.
- **Fixes.** A rare graphics crash (the screen freezing, then the game closing) is fixed. The panel that flickered at the bottom right of the map is gone (and similar one-frame flickers in other menus). Clicking the journal tab that's already open no longer closes the journal.

## Console (playtest builds)

Press the **`** key (left of 1, under Esc) for the console: for example `WRGive wood 50`, `WRHour 12`, `WRSpawn cinderfang 1`. In this playtest build anyone in the game can use it. README.txt beside the game lists more.

## Playing with friends

1. Start Wildreach, type a name, pick Male or Female, and Fire (volcanic) or Frost (tundra).
2. One person clicks **Host a game**. Everyone else types the host's IP and clicks **Join**.
   - Same Wi-Fi: the host's local IP (`ipconfig`, "IPv4 Address").
   - Over the internet: the host forwards UDP port 7777 on their router to their PC and shares their public IP.
3. Every key can be changed in Esc > Controls, and H shows them in game. To start: LMB / R attack, RMB block, C dodge, E interact (hold E on your tame for orders), 1–8 items, hold Q for abilities, I J P K M the journal, B build, X demolish, G a ranged weapon (RMB aims), Esc the menu, F10 back to the main menu.
