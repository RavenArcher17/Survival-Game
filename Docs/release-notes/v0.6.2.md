Everyone in a game needs the same version, so make sure your friends update too.

## New in 0.6.2: wildlife spawns by region

- **The island is split into six regions**: the volcanic and tundra sides, each in its lowland, midland and highland. Each region has its own creatures and a number of animals it keeps up.
- **Animals come back as new packs, out of sight.** About once a minute, a region that's short of animals gets a new pack or two, somewhere at least 300 m from every player and 100 m from anything built, and never inside a land claim. Herds no longer grow back where you thinned them, and they don't wander in from elsewhere.
- **Smaller packs.** Cinderfang, Gloomhowl, Ashstrider, Emberhorn and Frostantler run in threes and fours (they could be up to 12). Most others come alone or in twos and threes. Nothing wild comes in more than four.
- **Your existing world carries on.** Packs bigger than their kind allows are cut down to size when it loads, and the regions top up any shortfall with new small packs over the next few minutes.

## Console (playtest builds)

Press the **`** key (left of 1, under Esc) for the console: for example `WRGive wood 50`, `WRHour 12`, `WRSpawn cinderfang 1`. In this playtest build anyone in the game can use it. README.txt beside the game lists more.

## Playing with friends

1. Start Wildreach, type a name, pick Male or Female, and Fire (volcanic) or Frost (tundra).
2. One person clicks **Host a game** and chooses a world. Everyone else types the host's IP and clicks **Join**.
   - Same Wi-Fi: the host's local IP (`ipconfig`, "IPv4 Address").
   - Over the internet: the host forwards UDP port 7777 on their router to their PC and shares their public IP.
3. Every key can be changed in Esc > Controls, and H shows them in game. To start: LMB / R attack, RMB block, C dodge, E interact (hold E on your tame for orders), 1–8 items, hold Q for abilities, Tab lock on, I J P K M the journal, B build, X demolish, G a ranged weapon (RMB aims), Esc the menu, F10 back to the main menu.
