**Download: [Wildreach-Setup.exe](https://github.com/RavenArcher17/Survival-Game/releases/latest/download/Wildreach-Setup.exe)** (Windows 10/11, 64-bit: a small program that downloads the game, about 3.6 GB, and installs it)

Already have Wildreach? Just start it from its shortcut: it updates itself to 0.8.0 and your saves are kept. This is a big update: from 0.7.1 it downloads the whole game this one time (about 3.6 GB); updates after it fetch only what changed again. New here? Double-click the installer: it installs to your user folder, no admin needed, adds Desktop and Start Menu shortcuts and starts the game. To remove it, use "Uninstall Wildreach" in the Start Menu.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Everyone in a game needs the same version, so make sure your friends update too.

## New in 0.8.0: the whole game

0.7.1 was the first playable: the Old Island's Fire and Frost lands. 0.8.0 is the full game.

- **Twelve lands.** Besides Fire (the volcanic south) and Frost (the frozen tundra): Storm peaks (Lightning), the Swamp (Blood and curse), the Ancient forest (Nature), the Graveyard (Necromancy), the Desert (Sun and sand), the Ocean and reefs (Tide), the Sky islands (Wind), the Fungal caverns (Spores), the Crystal caves (Arcane) and the Corrupted wasteland (Void). Each has its own ground, places, caves, weather, hazards, creatures, bosses, quests, shrines, building set, armour set and music.
- **Twelve paths**, each with its own skill tree, abilities, resource and combos, and a look that marks your body as you go deeper.
- **The hero.** Faces, builds and armour looks you pick on the main menu; every biome's armour set on the body; path marks on the skin.
- **Combat.** Every weapon class, blocks, parries and crits checked on the server, stance and poise, clear attack tells, projectiles that fly true, and boss fights with phases and arenas in every land.
- **Survival.** Eating and drinking in the hand, waterskins you fill, sleep and comfort, weather, darkness and light, and the body's warnings.
- **Crafting.** Hands-on crafting, the workshop at work, crafting straight from your chests, metals up to starsteel, rings, talismans, runes, salvage and blueprints.
- **Building.** Metal and twelve biome building sets, beams and posts that must hold up, comfort decor, turrets, machines and power, siege engines (ram, trebuchet, ballista) and two strongholds to take.
- **Taming.** Levels and bond trees, saddles and barding, ground, swimming and flying mounts, the Stable, breeding, eggs and babies.
- **The world.** A fill-in map, landmarks and discoveries, shrines and fast travel, villages and merchants, path masters and their quests, invasions between lands, world events, dungeon portals and artifacts.
- **Multiplayer.** World passwords, player caps, admins, kick and ban, clans with a players list, chat channels, doors, claims, beds and raids. A dedicated server hosts 32 players.
- **Endgame.** Prestige, soul forging, mythic rules, boards, deeds, titles, cosmetics, monuments and the Thaw Hall raid.
- **Controller** support everywhere, a full settings menu, and a game that picks graphics levels for your PC the first time it starts.

## Playing with friends

1. Start Wildreach, type a name, and pick your look and your path.
2. One person clicks **Host a game** and chooses a world. Everyone else types the host's IP and clicks **Join**.
   - Same Wi-Fi: the host's local IP (`ipconfig`, "IPv4 Address").
   - Over the internet: the host forwards UDP port 7777 on their router to their PC and shares their public IP.
3. Every key can be changed in Esc > Controls, and F1 shows them in game.
