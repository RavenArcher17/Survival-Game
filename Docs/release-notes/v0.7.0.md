**Download: [Wildreach-Setup.exe](https://github.com/RavenArcher17/Survival-Game/releases/latest/download/Wildreach-Setup.exe)** (1.7 GB, Windows 10/11, 64-bit)

Already have Wildreach? Just start it from its shortcut: it updates itself to 0.7.0 and your saves are kept. From 0.6.1 or 0.6.2 it only downloads what changed, about {{SIZE}}, and from 0.6.2 it comes down over four connections at once; from 0.6.0 or earlier it's the whole game this one last time. New here? Double-click the installer: it installs to your user folder, no admin needed, adds Desktop and Start Menu shortcuts and starts the game. To remove it, use "Uninstall Wildreach" in the Start Menu.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Everyone in a game needs the same version, so make sure your friends update too.

## New in 0.7.0: creature raids

- **Creatures now raid your base.** A new world setting, **Creature raids** (Off, Rare, Normal or Frequent), on the New world form and in Esc > World. A claim is raided about every 72, 24 or 12 in-game hours, and only while someone of it (the owner or their clan) is within 250 m: nobody's base is raided while they're away.
- **A minute's warning** goes to everyone of the claim, wherever they are: a card with the raiders' picture, how many and from which way. Then they come from outside the claim's edge.
- **The raiders are the hunters of the base's region:** Cinderfangs, Scorchwings and Magmaws on the volcanic side, Gloomhowls, Glacimaws and Rimeclaws on the tundra side. A bigger Hearth draws bigger raids: 3 hunters' worth with no Hearth, 5 at level 1, 9 at level 3.
- **They go for your buildings** (walls, doors, chests and stations first) and fight anyone who comes near. Kill them all and you've held; after 6 minutes the rest give up and leave.
- **A raid tracker** under your level shows the countdown until they come, then how many are left.
- **A broken chest drops what was in it** in a bag anyone can pick up (it used to lose everything).

## Crafting takes time now

- **Station recipes take their time**: 1 to 3.5 seconds each on the Playtest and Casual co-op settings, twice that on the others. An order of several is made one after another. Recipes by hand are still instant.
- **The station keeps working when you walk off.** Each item goes into your bag while you're within 20 m; otherwise it waits in the station's **Ready** tray for **Take all**, and you're told when your order is done.
- **Each station queues up to 6 orders.** The strip under the recipes shows them with a bar for the one being made, and **Cancel** gives back what wasn't made yet. At a shared station, someone else's order says whose it is.
- **Queues and trays are saved with the station.** A smashed or demolished station drops its tray and the materials still in its orders in a bag. A broken Hearth pauses its stations' queues.

## Bosses

- **Bosses chase you properly.** They used to stop chasing you about 25 m from where they stood and then teleport back. Now a big boss chases you up to 90 m from its home and a smaller one up to 65 m. If everyone gets away, it walks back home instead of jumping there.

## Also

- **Friends who join see the whole island**, far peaks and all, as the host always did (they used to load only about 1 km around them).
- **The ice is now a glassy sheet** that follows the ground: deep blue where it's thick, frosted at the edges. Where it's slippery hasn't changed.
- **Fixed a crash** when the host changed the world's settings with a friend in the game.
- **The Crafting tab fits 4:3 and 5:4 screens better.** Notices no longer show through the big boss and raid cards.

## Also in this update: your base (0.6.3)

- **The Hearth and the land claim totem have their own Claim tab in the build bar.** They were the last two of eight cards on the Stations tab, and the bar only had room for three, so they sat out of sight past a scroll bar you could barely see.
- **Every build bar tab now pages its cards.** Round arrows on the ends of the cards (or the mouse wheel over them) turn to the next three, and a tag says where you are ("4–6 of 6"). The tab names shrink to fit on narrower screens.
- **A hint after your first claim totem** says where the Hearth is and what it's for.
- **A broken Hearth can be repaired.** Press E on it and click **Repair**: it costs up to half of what it took to build, less if it's only lightly damaged. A broken Hearth stands, its stations stop until you mend it, and its cores are never lost.
- **Name your chests.** **Rename** beside the chest's name opens a box; Enter or Save keeps the name. Only its owner and their clan can.
- **Waypoints.** Right-click on the map to set one; right-click it again to clear it. It shows on the map and on the compass with its distance.
- **Your base stays on the map from anywhere**, for friends who join your game too.

## Console (playtest builds)

Press the **`** key (left of 1, under Esc) for the console: for example `WRGive wood 50`, `WRHour 12`, `WRSpawn cinderfang 1`, `WRCmd raid now` (a raid on the nearest claim). In this playtest build anyone in the game can use it. README.txt beside the game lists more.

## Playing with friends

1. Start Wildreach, type a name, pick Male or Female, and Fire (volcanic) or Frost (tundra).
2. One person clicks **Host a game** and chooses a world. Everyone else types the host's IP and clicks **Join**.
   - Same Wi-Fi: the host's local IP (`ipconfig`, "IPv4 Address").
   - Over the internet: the host forwards UDP port 7777 on their router to their PC and shares their public IP.
3. Every key can be changed in Esc > Controls, and H shows them in game. To start: LMB / R attack, RMB block, C dodge, E interact (hold E on your tame for orders), 1–8 items, hold Q for abilities, Tab lock on, I J P K M the journal, B build, X demolish, G a ranged weapon (RMB aims), Esc the menu, F10 back to the main menu.
