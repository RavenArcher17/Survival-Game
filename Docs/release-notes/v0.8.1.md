**Download: [Wildreach-Setup.exe](https://github.com/RavenArcher17/Survival-Game/releases/latest/download/Wildreach-Setup.exe)** (Windows 10/11, 64-bit: a small program that downloads the game and installs it)

Already have Wildreach? Start it from its shortcut: it updates itself to 0.8.1 and your saves are kept (it fetches only what changed). Or click **Update now** on the main menu: Wildreach closes, installs the update and starts again by itself. New here? Double-click the installer: it installs to your user folder, no admin needed, adds Desktop and Start Menu shortcuts and starts the game. To remove it, use "Uninstall Wildreach" in the Start Menu.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Everyone in a game needs the same version, so make sure your friends update too.

## New in 0.8.1

- **Every screen fits your screen.** The main menu, every menu and the HUD now lay out right on 16:9, 16:10, 21:9 and 4:3 screens, from 1280x720 to 4K, at any Windows display scaling and any interface size. Nothing runs off the edge: Host a game, Join, Settings and Quit are always on screen.
- **The main menu is tidier.** The twelve paths sit in a short grid and the line under it names the land you wake in. When you change your look, your survivor stands in the open space beside the menu, head to toe, never behind a panel.
- **Update now just works.** It closes the game, installs the new version and starts it again. It never opens a web page.
- **The Hearth and Crafting screens** keep everything inside their windows (the Hearth's levels and Crafting's categories scroll; the boss cores sit in neat rows).
- **Clan map pins.** Share map pins with your clan; the legend's "Clan pins" shows theirs.
- **Places on the Old Island** that moved now sit where the land makes sense for them.
- **Strongholds** are proper forts: an outpost, a gate, a yard and a keep, held by a garrison of soldiers, archers, horn-blowers and hounds, with a captain, a keep warden and a warlord. Take the warlord down and the fort is yours.
- **Dedicated servers** take up to 100 players, with a join queue when a world is full.

## Playing with friends

1. Start Wildreach, type a name, and pick your look and your path.
2. One person clicks **Host a game** and chooses a world. Everyone else types the host's IP and clicks **Join**.
   - Same Wi-Fi: the host's local IP (`ipconfig`, "IPv4 Address").
   - Over the internet: the host forwards UDP port 7777 on their router to their PC and shares their public IP.
3. Every key can be changed in Esc > Controls, and F1 shows them in game.
