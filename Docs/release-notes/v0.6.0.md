**Download: [Wildreach-Setup.exe](https://github.com/RavenArcher17/Survival-Game/releases/latest/download/Wildreach-Setup.exe)** (1.7 GB, Windows 10/11, 64-bit)

Already have Wildreach? Just start it from its shortcut: it updates itself to 0.6.0 (your saves are kept). New here? Double-click the installer: it installs to your user folder, no admin needed, adds Desktop and Start Menu shortcuts and starts the game. To remove it, use "Uninstall Wildreach" in the Start Menu.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Everyone in a game needs the same version, so make sure your friends update too.

**Your old game comes with you.** The first time you click **Host a game**, your save is copied into a world called **Island**, with your character and everything you built. The old files stay where they were as a backup. Your bed comes too, but older versions never saved which bed you'd set, so walk up to it and set it once more with **E**.

## New in 0.6.0: a living island

- **Wildlife that roams.** Packs keep living when nobody is near: herds graze and move between feeding grounds, predators keep a territory and hunt, and some animals travel far. You meet them wherever they've got to, from a lone animal to a herd of twelve. Burrowers and colonies stay home.
- **A death is a death.** Thin out a herd and it stays thin, and hurt animals stay hurt for a while, even after a restart. Numbers come back slowly as they breed and as new packs wander in. Predators hunt out of your sight too, so a herd can shrink without you.
- **Worlds.** **Host a game** now opens **Choose a world**: your worlds, with when you last played and for how long, and buttons for Play, Rename, Delete and New world. Each world has its own buildings, tames, clans and characters, so everyone starts fresh in a new one.
- **World settings.** Pick PvE, PvP, Casual co-op or Playtest for a new world, then change anything you like: PvP, what happens when you die, raid protection, clan size, XP, gathering, taming and crop speeds, day length, hunger and thirst, creature numbers and difficulty, upkeep and boss respawn. The host can change them while playing, under **Esc > World**.
- **Choose where to wake.** When you die you look at the island map, with your beds, your clan's beds, the camps and a pin where you fell. Click one and press **Wake**. Beds and bedrolls you set now survive restarts. A bed rests for 60 seconds after you use it, and a bedroll is used up.
- **Bigger, better creatures.** Every creature was reviewed and resized: mounts and haulers tower over you now, and the Woolly drudge is a giant. They have new animation with feet that plant, on slopes too, four legs unless the real animal has more, and better surfaces. Several bosses were rebuilt. Ignessa has a real face, hair and wings; Grimjaw has real fur.

## Console (playtest builds)

Press the **`** key (left of 1, under Esc) for the console: for example `WRGive wood 50`, `WRHour 12`, `WRSpawn cinderfang 1`. In this playtest build anyone in the game can use it. README.txt beside the game lists more.

## Playing with friends

1. Start Wildreach, type a name, pick Male or Female, and Fire (volcanic) or Frost (tundra).
2. One person clicks **Host a game** and chooses a world. Everyone else types the host's IP and clicks **Join**.
   - Same Wi-Fi: the host's local IP (`ipconfig`, "IPv4 Address").
   - Over the internet: the host forwards UDP port 7777 on their router to their PC and shares their public IP.
3. Every key can be changed in Esc > Controls, and H shows them in game. To start: LMB / R attack, RMB block, C dodge, E interact (hold E on your tame for orders), 1–8 items, hold Q for abilities, I J P K M the journal, B build, X demolish, G a ranged weapon (RMB aims), Esc the menu, F10 back to the main menu.
