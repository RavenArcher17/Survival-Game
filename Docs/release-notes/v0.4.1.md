**Download: [Wildreach-Setup.exe](https://github.com/RavenArcher17/Survival-Game/releases/latest/download/Wildreach-Setup.exe)** (1.7 GB, Windows 10/11, 64-bit)

Already have Wildreach? Just start it from its shortcut: it updates itself to 0.4.1 (your saves are kept). New here? Double-click the installer: it installs in about 20 seconds (to your user folder, no admin needed), adds Desktop and Start Menu shortcuts and starts the game. To remove it, use "Uninstall Wildreach" in the Start Menu.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Everyone in a game needs the same version, so make sure your friends update too.

## Fixes in 0.4.1

- **The menus take clicks again.** In 0.4.0 an invisible layer from the new loading and death screens sat over every menu and caught the mouse, so you couldn't click a tab, a recipe, the Craft button or any setting (keys like I, J, B and Esc still opened and closed them). Everything can be clicked again, whether you host or join.
- **A menu left open under the chat box keeps its cursor.** Closing chat while a menu was open used to hide the cursor and turn the camera with the mouse until you closed the menu.
- **Cave creatures are back.** The Glacimaws and Kilnmites that lie in wait in caves used to fall through the world while buried, so caves were empty. They now wait where they are and come out when you get close.

Everything else is as in [0.4.0, the polish pass](https://github.com/RavenArcher17/Survival-Game/releases/tag/v0.4.0): sound, real animation, hits you can feel, magic effects, a better-looking island, death and loading screens, and smoother play.

## Playing with friends

1. Start Wildreach, type a name, pick Male or Female, and Fire (volcanic) or Frost (tundra).
2. One person clicks **Host a game**. Everyone else types the host's IP and clicks **Join**.
   - Same Wi-Fi: the host's local IP (`ipconfig`, "IPv4 Address").
   - Over the internet: the host forwards UDP port 7777 on their router to their PC and shares their public IP.
3. Every key can be changed in Esc > Controls, and H shows them in game. To start: LMB / R attack, RMB block, C dodge, E interact (hold E on your tame for orders), 1–8 items, hold Q for abilities, I J P K M the journal, B build, G a ranged weapon (RMB aims), Esc the menu, F10 back to the main menu.
