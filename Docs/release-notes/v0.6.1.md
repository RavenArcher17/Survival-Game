Already have Wildreach? Just start it from its shortcut: it updates itself to 0.6.1 (your saves are kept). New here? Double-click the installer: it installs to your user folder, no admin needed, adds Desktop and Start Menu shortcuts and starts the game. To remove it, use "Uninstall Wildreach" in the Start Menu.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Everyone in a game needs the same version, so make sure your friends update too.

## New in 0.6.1: aiming and building

- **A crosshair.** A small mark at the middle of the screen, red over anything hostile. You can turn it off in Esc > Controls.
- **Spells go where you aim.** They used to fly beside the crosshair: they now go through it, and Lava pool and Meteor land on it.
- **Lock-on (Tab) hits what's moving.** Locked on, spells aim ahead of the target and curve toward it, and Meteor lands where it's heading. What's locked gets gold corners around it.
- **Lock-on only takes what you can see.** It used to lock on to buried creatures, so it could point you at a bare patch of cave or a lava pool. It also won't lock through walls, and it lets go if the target goes underground or stays out of sight.
- **Pieces follow you when you build.** A piece now faces the way you look, instead of keeping the angle it had when you picked it.
- **Turn pieces to any angle.** With nothing to snap to, tap **R** to turn a piece, hold it to spin, and use the mouse wheel for fine turns.
- **Finer snapping.** **F** now goes Grid, Fine, Off. Furniture on a foundation or floor snaps to marks on it and sits square to it. Fine adds smaller steps, walls on inner lines and pillars where the lines cross. A foundation placed at an angle keeps its own grid for whatever you build on it and beside it.
- **See what you're placing.** The Armorer, Forge, Workbench, Hearth and the rest show their real shapes when you place them.

## Much smaller updates, from the next one on

Updates used to download the whole game every time. **After this one, your game only fetches what changed**: about 235 MB when the game's code changes, and under a megabyte for a balance patch. This update is still the full 1.7 GB for you, because it's what delivers the new updater. If a small update can't be done for any reason, it falls back to downloading the whole game, as before.

## Console (playtest builds)

Press the **`** key (left of 1, under Esc) for the console: for example `WRGive wood 50`, `WRHour 12`, `WRSpawn cinderfang 1`. In this playtest build anyone in the game can use it. README.txt beside the game lists more.

## Playing with friends

1. Start Wildreach, type a name, pick Male or Female, and Fire (volcanic) or Frost (tundra).
2. One person clicks **Host a game** and chooses a world. Everyone else types the host's IP and clicks **Join**.
   - Same Wi-Fi: the host's local IP (`ipconfig`, "IPv4 Address").
   - Over the internet: the host forwards UDP port 7777 on their router to their PC and shares their public IP.
3. Every key can be changed in Esc > Controls, and H shows them in game. To start: LMB / R attack, RMB block, C dodge, E interact (hold E on your tame for orders), 1–8 items, hold Q for abilities, Tab lock on, I J P K M the journal, B build, X demolish, G a ranged weapon (RMB aims), Esc the menu, F10 back to the main menu.
