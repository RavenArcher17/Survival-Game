**Download: [Wildreach-Setup.exe](https://github.com/RavenArcher17/Survival-Game/releases/latest/download/Wildreach-Setup.exe)** (1.7 GB, Windows 10/11, 64-bit)

Already have Wildreach? Just start it from its shortcut: it updates itself to 0.4.0 (your saves are kept). New here? Double-click the installer: it installs in about 20 seconds (to your user folder, no admin needed), adds Desktop and Start Menu shortcuts and starts the game. To remove it, use "Uninstall Wildreach" in the Start Menu.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Everyone in a game needs the same version, so make sure your friends update too.

## New in 0.4.0: the polish pass

No new content this time: everything already in the game now looks, sounds and moves like a finished game.

- **It has sound.** Footsteps that change with the ground, swords, blocks and parries, creature calls for every species, gathering, building, spells and bows, menus, and ambience that follows where you are and the time of day: volcanic rumble, tundra wind, surf, birds by day, insects and owls at night, caves. Music for the menu, for exploring, for fights and for bosses. Volumes are in Settings > Audio.
- **Real animation.** You can block, swing, cast and aim while walking or running; strafing and walking backward look right; every move blends smoothly; you turn on the spot and your feet sit on slopes. Creatures turn instead of spinning, their feet keep pace with the ground, and lunges, charges, stomps and spit each look different.
- **Hits you can feel.** A short hit-stop and camera shake on heavy blows and parries, sparks and blood where hits land, a parry flare, a hit marker, and gold damage numbers for criticals.
- **Magic that looks like magic.** Fire and frost bolts with trails and light, real fire walls, lava pools, frost novas and ice lances. Wood chips when you chop, dust when you build, flames on Hearths and torches, embers over lava, snow on the tundra and ash on the volcanic side.
- **A better-looking island.** Moving sea with waves and foam, a warm ash haze on the volcanic side and cold blue air on the tundra, light shafts at dawn and dusk, mist in the valleys. Walls are planks and coursed stone, roofs have shingles and slates, and the workbench, forge, armorer, alchemy table, Hearth, claim totem and chest are real models. Boss attack warnings glow on the ground.
- **The moments in between.** A death screen that tells you what killed you, where your belongings are and where you'll wake (with a compass marker back to your bag); a loading screen with tips; banners when you level up, find a camp, cave or boss lair, unlock recipes or tame a creature; a few one-time hints with your own keys (switch them off in Settings > Video). Menus fade in and buttons react.
- **Smoother.** No more stutters on the walk past the first camp and herd, and hosts with lots of wildlife around run much lighter. On a mid-high PC it runs around 120 fps at 1080p on Epic.

## Fixes

- Boss arenas no longer have plain white blocks: Vulkarn's anvils are stone and Skaldrith's pillars are ice.

## Playing with friends

1. Start Wildreach, type a name, pick Male or Female, and Fire (volcanic) or Frost (tundra).
2. One person clicks **Host a game**. Everyone else types the host's IP and clicks **Join**.
   - Same Wi-Fi: the host's local IP (`ipconfig`, "IPv4 Address").
   - Over the internet: the host forwards UDP port 7777 on their router to their PC and shares their public IP.
3. Every key can be changed in Esc > Controls, and H shows them in game. To start: LMB / R attack, RMB block, C dodge, E interact (hold E on your tame for orders), 1–8 items, hold Q for abilities, I J P K M the journal, B build, G a ranged weapon (RMB aims), Esc the menu, F10 back to the main menu.
