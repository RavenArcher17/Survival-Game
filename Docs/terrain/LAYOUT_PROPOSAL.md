# First Playable landmass: approved layout

Your Map tab said the layout needed more planning toward a realistic landmass (natural coastlines, terrain,
rivers, mountain range shape) before it's built. This is that step. **Approved by Raven on 2026-10-23**
(decisions below); next is the full-resolution export and the Unreal import.

![layout](layout_proposal.png)

Generated by `Tools/terrain/layout_proposal.mjs` (seed 7; any change is a quick rerun). Numbers in
`layout_proposal.json`.

## What you're looking at

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Brown (west) / white (east) | Volcanic / Frozen tundra, split by the ridge |
| Shade bands | Outer lowlands → midlands → highlands (darker / bluer toward the ridge) |
| Grey band | The ridge: cliffs too steep to climb (over 35°) |
| Yellow rings | North and south passes, the only walkable crossings (verified by a slope check) |
| Red dots | Spawn points / safe zones, 3 per outer edge |
| Purple rings | Major boss arenas: Vulkarn in the volcano's crater, Skaldrith on the glacier plateau |
| Orange dots | Mini-boss sites in the midlands (Obsidian Warden, Ignessa, Grimjaw, Pale Hunter) |
| Blue line, west | Water river (steaming, drinkable) |
| Orange line, west | Lava river (gear check to cross) |
| Pale blue lines, east | Icy rivers |

## The numbers

| | Value |
|---|---|
| Map | 4 × 4 km, about **9.9 km² of land** (the rest is sea around the island) |
| Highest point | ~880 m (ridge peaks); passes drop to ~110 m |
| Spawn → nearest pass, walking | **3.7–4.3 min** for the corner spawns, **5.6 min** for the middle spawns |
| Walkable ridge crossings | exactly two (north and south passes) |

## Decisions (2026-10-23)

| # | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Island or mainland? | **Island**: every edge is coastline. |
| 2 | Middle spawns ~7 min from a pass | **Moved diagonally toward a pass** (west middle toward the north pass, east middle toward the south pass, so both passes get traffic). Now 5.6 min; closer would put them in the midlands or on top of the corner spawns. |
| 3 | Ridge: hard wall or climbs? | **Hard wall**: only the two passes cross it. |
| 4 | Boss sites | **As proposed**: Vulkarn in the volcano's crater, Skaldrith in the glacier plateau's bowl. |
| 5 | Volcanic rivers | **One of each**: the north river is water, the south river is lava. |
| 6 | Island size | **~10 km² for the first playtest.** It needs to grow once all the biomes are added. |
