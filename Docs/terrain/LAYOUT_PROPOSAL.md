## Questions for you

1. **Island or mainland?** I made it an island so every edge is a natural coastline. The alternative is land running off the map edges, with mountains or cliffs as the boundary.
2. **Middle spawns are ~7 minutes from a pass**, versus your "about 5 minutes". Options: move the middle spawns diagonally toward a pass, add a third pass in the middle, or accept 7.
3. **The ridge is a continuous cliff wall** (~600 m high). Should it have climbable spots for skilled players, or stay a hard wall with only the two passes?
4. **Boss sites:** Vulkarn in the volcano's crater (a natural "forge cavern" entrance) and Skaldrith on the glacier plateau's bowl. OK?
5. **Volcanic rivers:** lava channels (dangerous, gear check) or ordinary water that steams? Water matters for thirst on that side.
6. **Island size:** about 10 km² of land. Bigger means more walking, smaller means more crowded on a 100-player server.
