# Creatures

Each first playable biome has 9 original fantasy creatures: 3 aggressive, 3 neutral and 3 passive. Every creature can be tamed, but some are much harder than others. Biomes don't need every role, so each one leans toward its own strengths.

**Rules for every creature**

- Creatures resist their own biome's hazards: volcanic creatures can cross lava, and tundra creatures move freely on ice.
- Taming methods are knockout-and-feed, offering food, stealing eggs, and path magic (using your path's element to calm a creature of the same element).
- Necromancers can raise any of these creatures after death, whatever their taming difficulty.
- Neutral herds defend each other; attack one and the rest turn hostile.

## Volcanic

The volcano leans toward fighters, haulers and utility. It has no swimmer.

| Creature | Behavior | Role | What it is | Taming | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Magmaw | Aggressive | Fighter | Lizard-like predator that swims through lava and bursts out of pools | Knockout and feed, or fire path magic | Hard |
| Cinderfang | Aggressive | Fighter | Six-legged ember hounds that hunt in packs | Knockout and feed | Medium |
| Scorchwing | Aggressive | Fighter, scout | Bat-winged fire flyer that dives from above | Knockout and feed (bring it down first) | Medium |
| Basaltback | Neutral | Hauler | Huge tortoise-like beast with a rock shell | Offer sulfur crystals | Medium |
| Emberhorn | Neutral | Mount | Charging beast with a glowing horn | Knockout and feed | Medium |
| Kilnmite | Neutral | Utility | Giant burrowing beetle that mines ore and senses veins; swarms if its nest is disturbed | Steal an egg | Easy |
| Ashstrider | Passive | Mount | Tall, long-legged grazer and the fastest ground mount | Offer food | Easy |
| Glowmoth | Passive | Utility | Giant moth that lights up caves | Offer nectar | Easy |
| Pumicehog | Passive | Farm animal | Small hopping critter raised for meat and fat | Offer food | Easy |

## Tundra

The tundra leans toward fighters, mounts and exploration. It has no cave light.

| Creature | Behavior | Role | What it is | Taming | Difficulty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rimeclaw | Aggressive | Fighter | Four-eyed saber-toothed predator with crystal spines | Knockout and feed, or frost path magic | Hard |
| Glacimaw | Aggressive | Fighter, utility | Ice worm that bursts out of the snow and tunnels through ice | Knockout and feed | Hard |
| Gloomhowl | Aggressive | Fighter | Pale, eyeless pack hunters that track by sound | Knockout and feed | Medium |
| Woolly drudge | Neutral | Hauler | Massive shaggy six-legged beast | Offer lichen | Medium |
| Frostantler | Neutral | Mount | Elk-like beast with ice-crystal antlers, fast on ice | Knockout and feed | Medium |
| Rimeshell | Neutral | Utility | Giant shelled creature that breaks ice and digs up ore | Steal an egg | Medium |
| Snowpuff | Passive | Farm animal | Fluffy flightless bird raised for eggs, meat and feathers | Offer food | Easy |
| Aurora fox | Passive | Utility | Small glowing fox that senses hidden areas and secrets | Offer food | Easy |
| Floeback | Passive | Swimming mount | Seal-like swimmer that carries a rider through icy water | Offer fish | Easy |

Gloomhowls track by sound, so the sneaking skill matters more in the tundra. Floeback is the first playable's only swimming mount; the build plan has swimming mounts in full release, so it waits until then with the other swimming mounts.
