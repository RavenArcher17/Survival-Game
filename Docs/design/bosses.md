# Bosses

The first playable has one major boss and two mini-bosses in each biome. Each major boss is weak to its opposite element, so fire players need frost (and frost players need fire) to fight their home boss easily. That pushes everyone toward a subclass.

**Rules for every boss**

- Major bosses have 2 phases. A hidden third phase appears only when the party has artifact modifiers active.
- Health scales with group size, and each tame counts as an extra player.
- Anyone who deals at least 10% of a boss's health gets their own loot.
- A major boss drops a boss core for the Hearth, path materials and a chance at its boss part.
- Mini-bosses drop upgrade shards for +1 to +10 upgrades, plus a trophy.

## Vulkarn, the Forge Giant (volcanic)

A molten giant blacksmith who works an endless forge with a burning hammer.

| Part | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Arena | A forge cavern with stone anvils raised above the floor. His big slams flood the floor with lava for a few seconds; standing on an anvil keeps you safe. |
| Phase 1 | Three-hit hammer combo; overhead slam that leaves lava cracks; tongs grab that catches careless players; anvil slam that floods the floor |
| Phase 2 (below 50%) | Armor glows white-hot; bellows breath in a cone; throws molten slag; floods more often; anvils begin to melt, so fewer platforms remain |
| Hidden phase 3 | Quenched rage: he plunges into the lava and returns with a second hammer; every slam floods the floor |
| Weakness | Frost. Parrying the tongs grab staggers him. |
| Drops | Boss core, fire path materials, boss part |
| Boss part | Giant's ember: staggering an enemy sends out a small lava shockwave |

## Skaldrith, the Frost Wyrm (tundra)

An ice dragon that rules a frozen mountain bowl.

| Part | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Arena | A frozen mountain bowl with tall ice pillars. Pillars block his breath, but his tail and charges shatter them. |
| Phase 1 | Frost breath sweep; claw swipes; tail sweep that breaks pillars; lunging bite |
| Phase 2 (below 50%) | Takes flight and dives; breath from the air turns the ground to slick ice; ice spikes burst from the floor; few pillars remain |
| Hidden phase 3 | Whiteout: a blizzard cuts visibility, he hides in the snow, and his breath freezes players solid on contact |
| Weakness | Fire. Hitting his wings while he flies grounds him. |
| Drops | Boss core, frost path materials, boss part |
| Boss part | Wyrm scale: blocking an attack can freeze the attacker |
