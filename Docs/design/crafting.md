# Crafting

Every base has one Hearth, tied to its land claim totem. Feeding it boss cores from major bosses raises its tech level, which sets the tier of every station in that claim. Anyone using those stations crafts at that tier.

**Rules**

- Four stations: forge, armorer, alchemy table and workbench. All of them level together through the Hearth.
- Each tech level needs cores from a set number of different major bosses, from any biomes. Mini-bosses don't count toward tech levels.
- Raiders can damage the Hearth. A broken Hearth stops crafting until it's repaired, but its cores stay safe.
- Ores are found only in mines and caves. Every biome has caves, and deeper caves hold better metal, so no biome is the "metal biome."
- One shared recipe tree: base metal sets the tier, a path material sets the element, and extras add bonuses.

## Tech levels

| Level | Different major bosses | Base metal | Upgrade cap | Forge | Armorer | Alchemy table | Workbench |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 | Copper | +2 | Copper weapons, first infused weapons | Hide and copper armor | Minor healing potion | Wood and stone building, basic tools, bedroll, storage |
| 2 | 1 | Bronze | +4 | Bronze weapons, bows | Bronze armor, shields | Healing potion, antidote, first brews | Locks, water wheel, saddles |
| 3 | 2 | Iron | +6 | Iron weapons, crossbows | Iron armor, creature armor | Status-resist potions, resource tonics | Windmill, power, conveyors, sorting, irrigation |
| 4 | 4 | Steel | +8 | Steel weapons, siege weapons | Steel armor, biome set bonuses | Greater healing, buff brews | Metal building, drills, turrets, greenhouses, boats |
| 5 | 6 | Starsteel | +10 | Starsteel weapons, legendary blueprints | Starsteel armor | Elixirs with rare effects | Biome-themed machines and building sets, production chains |

Starsteel is forged with boss cores. The first playable has two major bosses, so it reaches level 3.

## Quality and sockets

Crafting skill, Hearth level and material rarity all raise the odds of better quality. Quality sets how many extras an item can hold. Stats also roll randomly within each quality.

| Quality | Sockets |
| --- | --- |
| Crude | 0 |
| Common | 1 |
| Fine | 2 |
| Masterwork | 3 |

## Extras

| Extra | Where it comes from | What it adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gems | Mines and caves | Stat bonuses such as damage, critical chance or poise |
| Runes | Crafted or found | Special effects such as lifesteal on hit or faster status buildup |
| Boss parts | Major boss drops | A unique effect tied to that boss |

Extras can be removed, but removing one destroys it.

## Upgrades

Weapons and armor upgrade from +1 to +10 using mini-boss drops only. Higher steps cost more drops. The Hearth's level caps how far an item can go, from +2 at level 1 to +10 at level 5.

## Other crafting

- Repairs happen at the matching station.
- Salvaging gear returns some materials. Socketed extras are destroyed.
- Crafting queues keep running while you're away.
- Blueprints from bosses and dungeon portals unlock special recipes at any level that meets their tier.
