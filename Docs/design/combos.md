# Combos

Every pair of paths has two automatic combos: one at 25/25 points and one at 50/50. They cost no points. All 66 pairs were picked from options in design review.

The Wheel column shows how the two paths sit on the biome wheel: opposites are straight across, neighbors are next to each other.

A combo that changes a specific ability (such as Storm volley or Lich form) only works once you have unlocked that ability.
