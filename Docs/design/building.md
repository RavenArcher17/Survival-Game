# Building

Building uses both snapping and freeform placement, inside land claims. The first playable has wood and stone; metal and biome materials arrive in full release.

## Land claims

- A land claim totem lets only your clan build within its radius.
- A new claim covers 30 m. Each totem upgrade adds 10 m, up to 60 m.
- Players and clans can place unlimited claims. Each claim pays its own upkeep, so more bases means more materials to keep them standing.
- Each claim can hold one Hearth, and each Hearth levels separately with its own boss cores.
- Upkeep is paid in building materials stocked in the totem, and it scales with the number of pieces in the claim. When it runs out, the claim drops and the base slowly decays.

## Materials and integrity

Stronger materials span farther before they need support from a foundation or pillar.

| Material | Unlocked at | Strength | Span before support |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wood | Hearth level 1 | Low | 4 pieces |
| Stone | Hearth level 1 | Medium | 6 pieces |
| Metal (full release) | Hearth level 4 | High | 8 pieces |
| Biome materials (full release) | Hearth level 5 | High, with a themed look | 8 pieces |

## Pieces in the first playable

| Type | Pieces |
| --- | --- |
| Core | Foundations, walls, half walls, floors, roofs, stairs, pillars, doors, windows |
| Defense | Palisade walls, gates, guard towers, reinforced doors with code locks |
| Utility | Storage chests, beds, bedrolls, the four crafting stations, the Hearth, torches and braziers, animal pens, crop plots |
| Decor | Furniture, rugs, banners, boss trophies; decor raises the comfort buff |

There are no spike or trap pieces, since traps were ruled out.
