# Life skills

Fifteen skills level from 1 to 100 by doing them. Each level gives a small boost, and at levels 25, 50, 75 and 100 you choose one of two perks. There is no total cap and skills never decay, so a player can master everything with time.

Life skills are separate from path points. All perks are first drafts.
