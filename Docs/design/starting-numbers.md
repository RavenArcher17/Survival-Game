# Starting numbers

These are first values to put into Unreal and tune in playtests. They aim for the feel you chose: regular mobs take 5–7 hits, a major boss takes a group of three 10–15 minutes, one path takes about 80 hours to max, and a life skill takes about 50 hours to reach level 100.

## Player

| Stat | Start | Growth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Health | 100 | +3 per character level, up to 250 at level 50 |
| Stamina | 100 | +1 per character level, up to 150 at level 50 |
| Stamina regen | 30 per second | Starts 0.8 seconds after you stop using stamina |
| Respawn time | 10 seconds |  |

| Action | Stamina cost |
| --- | --- |
| Light attack | 10 |
| Heavy attack | 20 |
| Dodge roll | 15 |
| Blocking a hit | 10–25, depending on the hit |
| Sprinting | 5 per second |

## Weapons

A copper sword does 12 damage with a light attack and 22 with a heavy attack. Everything else multiplies from there.

| Factor | Multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| Copper / bronze / iron / steel / starsteel | 1.0 / 1.3 / 1.7 / 2.2 / 2.8 |
| Crude / common / fine / masterwork | 0.9 / 1.0 / 1.1 / 1.2 |
| Each upgrade level (+1 to +10) | +5% |

## Enemies

| Enemy | Health | Hit damage | Hits to kill it at expected gear |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lowland creature | 70 | 12 | About 6 with copper |
| Midland creature | 120 | 20 | About 6 with upgraded bronze |
| Highland creature | 200 | 32 | 6–7 with upgraded iron |
| Mini-boss (solo) | 3,000 | 25–45 | A 3–4 minute fight |
| Major boss (solo) | 12,000 | 30–60 | Gets health from group size |

Boss health grows by 80% for each extra player or tame, so a group of three faces about 31,000, roughly a 12-minute fight.

## Progression

| System | Starting rule | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Path points | Point number n costs 100 + 10n path XP, about 60,500 XP for all 100 | About 80 hours of play per path |
| Life skills | Level n costs 50 + 5n XP, about 30,000 XP to reach 100 | About 50 hours per skill |
| Character level | Level = total path points spent ÷ 3, so 150 points is level 50 | Level 50 when both paths are at the 150-point cap |

## Survival and taming

| System | Starting value |
| --- | --- |
| Hunger | Full to empty in 60 minutes of play |
| Thirst | Full to empty in 40 minutes of play |
| Empty hunger or thirst | Stamina regen halves, then health slowly drains |
| Taming time (taming skill 1) | Easy 1 minute, medium 5 minutes, hard 15 minutes |
| Taming skill effect | Up to 50% faster at level 100 |
| Crop growth | Common crops 20 minutes, rare crops 2 hours (real time) |
