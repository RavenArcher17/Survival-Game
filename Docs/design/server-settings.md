# Server settings

Official servers run one of three locked presets. Community servers can change almost anything. The preset values below are first drafts to tune in playtests.

## Presets

| Setting | Options | PvP standard | PvE | Casual co-op |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PvP | On or off | On | Off | Off |
| Death penalty | Gentle, Souls-like, drop inventory, full loot | Souls-like | Souls-like | Gentle |
| Offline base protection | 0–100% less damage | 75% | Not needed | Not needed |
| PvP inside boss arenas | On or off | On | Not needed | Not needed |
| Bounties and infamy | On or off | On | Off | Off |
| Clan size limit | 2–50 or no limit | 8 | 12 | No limit |
| XP and skill gain | 0.5×–10× | 1× | 1× | 2× |
| Gathering and drops | 0.5×–10× | 1× | 1× | 2× |
| Taming and breeding speed | 0.5×–10× | 1× | 1× | 3× |
| Crop and crafting timers | 0.5×–10× faster | 1× | 1× | 2× |
| Boss respawn time | 1–24 real hours | 4 hours | 4 hours | 2 hours |
| Base upkeep cost | 0.5×–3× | 1× | 1× | 0.5× |
| Creature raids on bases | Off, rare, normal, frequent | Normal | Normal | Rare |

## Community servers

Community server owners can change every setting above and almost any other value in the game, such as creature difficulty, day length or tame limits. A short list of rules stays fixed on every server, so the game still works as designed:

- The 10% damage rule for boss loot
- The 150-point path cap, with no path above 100
- Weird rules from artifact modifiers apply only to the wearer

## Full release settings

These join the list when their systems ship: dungeon portal break timers, artifact modifier limits, biome invasion frequency and raid group sizes.
