# Map

The first playable map is about 4 × 4 km. A central mountain ridge splits it, with the volcano on the west face and the glacier on the east face.

> **Note for the build:** this layout is a rough plan. Before building the map in Unreal, it needs more planning to become a realistic landmass, with natural coastlines, terrain, rivers and the shape of the mountain range.

&#91;embedded content: first playable map · about 4 × 4 km\]

| Area | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| Outer lowlands | Three spawn points on each outer edge, which are also safe zones. Easy creatures. |
| Midlands | Mini-bosses, most caves and ore, medium creatures |
| Highlands | The major bosses against the ridge and the toughest creatures |
| Ridge | Blocks travel between the biomes |
| North and south passes | The contested borderland, and the only way across. Creatures from both biomes fight here, invasions come through, and it's the likely PvP hotspot. |

Walking from a spawn to the nearest pass takes about 5 minutes; a mount makes it much faster.
